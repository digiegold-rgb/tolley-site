Try an answer without posting

A preview never sends chat or DMs. Try a follow-up such as “What condition is it?” The local model interprets the question; replies use recorded facts only.

Inventory connection

Catalog refreshes every minute. Sold/archived records override old active listings. Drafts are leads for the host to check, not proof of available stock. Internal costs and floor prices are never loaded.

This selects a saved Tolley lineup. It is not an automatic scrape of tonight’s Whatnot auctions. Reconfirm it for each new show. Questions like “this one” need a named product in that viewer’s recent conversation; the bot cannot see what you are holding.

Connect and start

One assistant handles greetings, thank-yous and inventory questions. Public messages have a 60-second gap between public messages and at most 40 public messages/hour. Product follow-ups can use the next available one-minute slot; repeated greetings wait five minutes. Starting or stopping an assistant never starts or ends your broadcast.

Keep the parts you like

Thank-you DMs wait 45 seconds by default. Your existing recipient cooldowns and opt-outs carry over.

Saved answers take precedence over catalog matching. Review and save after connecting each show. Shipping, guarantees, compatibility and uncertain condition questions stay with you unless you provide a verified answer.
