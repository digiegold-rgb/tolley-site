Ruthann Marketplace two-message automation

Spark owns the worker and private state; Mac mini owns the Beeper API token.
Account: Ruthann personal Facebook only. No Grok or Page messages.
Commands on Spark:
  beeper-sales status
  beeper-sales pause
  beeper-sales resume
  beeper-sales pause 'CHAT_ID'
  beeper-sales resume 'CHAT_ID'
  beeper-sales listing-pause 'Exact listing title without buyer prefix'
  beeper-sales listing-resume 'Exact listing title without buyer prefix'
  beeper-sales reset 'CHAT_ID'
  beeper-sales preview
Status includes chat IDs and titles. Listing pauses apply to current/future matching titles.
Conversation reset keeps processed-message history; it allows the two steps for NEW messages again.
Only reset an uncertain-send conversation after checking Messenger for delivery.
State: ~/.config/tolley-beeper-automation/state.sqlite (private).
Service: systemctl --user status tolley-beeper-sales
Logs: journalctl --user -u tolley-beeper-sales
Emergency stop: systemctl --user stop tolley-beeper-sales

Rules: day reply until 19:00 America/Chicago; tomorrow reply 19:00-23:59.
ETA reply follows positive buyer interest after the first reply, once per chat.
Human replies pause that chat. Incomplete identity or uncertain writes fail closed.
Assumes availability until paused. Does not check inventory, holidays, or reservations.
Messages older than one hour after an outage are held. Historical imports are ignored.
Eligibility uses account ID, buyer/item title, and exactly two participants including self.
Threads where Ruthann asks availability herself are held as buyer-side conversations.
Ambiguous names/titles require review; this is not a universal Marketplace detector.
Connection down => error visible in status; no fallback to other accounts.
Keep Beeper open on mini. Never print or move its token into chat or repository.
No external LLM or new paid dependency.

HQ inbox extension:
  Owner dashboard: /hq/inbox and HQ > Chats.
  Worker needs CUSTOMER_SYNC_SECRET and optional CUSTOMER_SYNC_URL (existing
  protected customer-sync environment file). No browser receives either token.
  Worker polls authenticated commands; snapshots sync roughly every 30 seconds.
  The recent message page is mirrored, not the entire historical archive.
  Pending controls display queued until acknowledged. Never claim queued means sent.
  Owner sends pause automation, require the latest message ID, and have a durable
  command ID. An uncertain send is never retried automatically.
  Notes, follow-ups, and explicit customer links remain in owner HQ. No automatic
  identity merging, lead harvesting, or real-estate replies are enabled.
  HqSyncState stores inbox snapshots, notes, command receipts and send guards;
  it is already included in the private customer backup. No schema migration.
  Connector health becomes stale after 90 seconds. Old snapshots remain readable.
  Review commands with uncertain/error status in HQ; verify Messenger before retrying.
  Do not delete SQLite command receipts: they prevent replay of acknowledged sends.
