# Jelly Studio Page — click path (Facebook, 2026 layout)

Do this from **the Page itself**, not from your personal profile. Top-right
avatar → **See all profiles** → switch to **Jelly Studio**. Everything below
assumes you are "acting as" the Page; if a menu doesn't match, you are still on
your personal profile.

All asset files are in `~/Shared/jelly-studio-brand/` on the Mac (and at
`https://www.tolley.io/animate/brand/<file>` once the site deploys). Text to
paste is in `COPY.md` next to this file.

> **Before you type anything of your own:** public copy leads with affordability
> and no commitment. Never put the cost mechanic ("compute at cost", "$0.35 per
> finished minute", any per-minute rate) on the Page, in a post, or in an ad —
> that lives only on the in-app receipt. Never say "cheapest in the industry"
> and never name a competitor. Full rules in `COPY.md` §7.

## 1. Profile picture (2 min)

1. Open the Page.
2. Hover the profile circle → the **camera icon** appears → click it.
3. **Upload photo** → choose `logo-1024.png`.
4. Facebook shows a circular crop tool. **Do not zoom in** — the mark is already
   composed for the circle. Drag until the wordmark is centred.
5. **Save**.
6. Facebook auto-creates a "changed profile picture" post. Leave it; it is your
   first bit of reach. Optionally add the caption from `COPY.md` post 1.

*The mark **is** the wordmark — JELLY / STUDIO stacked on brand pink — so there
is nothing extra to add beside it. Use `logo-1024.png` for the Page picture
(Facebook shows it at 176px, where the TOLLEY.IO line still reads). Use
`logo-compact-512.png` anywhere under 128px — favicons, small avatars — because
the TOLLEY.IO line stops being legible there. `logo-mono-black.png` /
`logo-mono-white.png` are the transparent one-colour stamps, and `logo.svg` is
the vector with the type already outlined.*

## 2. Cover photo (2 min)

1. Hover the wide banner area → **Add cover photo** (or **Edit cover photo**).
2. **Upload photo** → `cover-1640x624.png`.
3. Drag to reposition — the design already keeps the important text inside
   Facebook's centred 1200×624 safe area and clear of the profile circle, so
   centring it is correct.
4. **Save changes**.
5. Open the Page **on your phone** and check the crop. If the headline is
   clipped, replace it with `cover-mobile-820x360.png` — that variant is
   centre-composed for the phone crop.

## 3. About / Edit details (5 min)

1. Left menu → **Settings** → **Page setup**, or on the Page: **Edit details**
   (sometimes shown as **Edit Page info**).
2. Fill in, from `COPY.md` §1 and §2:
   - **Name:** `Jelly Studio`
   - **Username:** try `jellystudio.ai`, then `jellystudiobytolley`, then
     `jellystudiovideo`. Facebook tells you inline if it is taken.
   - **Category:** type `Software company`, pick it from the dropdown.
   - **Bio:** the ≤255-char short bio.
   - **Additional information / description:** the long description.
   - **Website:** `https://www.tolley.io/animate`
   - **Email:** `support@tolley.io`
   - **Hours:** *Always open*.
3. **Save**. The username change may take a minute to propagate.

## 4. Action button (1 min)

1. On the Page, the blue button under the cover reads **Add action button** (or
   shows an existing one with a pencil).
2. Click it → choose **Learn more**.
3. It asks where the button leads → **Website** → paste
   `https://www.tolley.io/animate`.
4. **Save**. Then click the button yourself once to confirm it opens the
   landing page and not a Facebook interstitial dead-end.

## 5. The four posts (15 min)

Post them in order, ideally over 4 days rather than all at once.

**Post 1 — image**
1. Page → **What's on your mind?** composer (make sure the avatar in the
   composer is the *Page*, not you).
2. **Photo/video** → `post-1-what-it-is.png`.
3. Paste caption from `COPY.md` §3, post 1.
4. **Post**.

**Post 2 — image:** same steps with `post-2-pricing.png` and its caption.

**Post 3 — video (the important one)**
1. Composer → **Photo/video** → `demo-money-mindset-30s.mp4`.
2. Paste the post-3 caption.
3. Before posting, open the video options and set:
   - **Thumbnail:** upload `poster-30s-1280x720.jpg` rather than letting
     Facebook pick a frame mid-blink.
   - **Title:** `The Quiet Exit — 30 second cut`
   - **AI-generated content disclosure:** **ON** (see §8).
4. **Post**.
5. Afterwards, also upload `demo-money-mindset-full.mp4` (the 3:24 version) with
   poster `poster-full-1280x720.jpg` — it gives the Videos tab something real
   and gives the ad landing experience a second thing to watch.

**Post 4 — image:** `post-4-invite.png` and its caption.

## 6. Pin post 1 (30 sec)

1. Find post 1 in the feed.
2. **⋯** (three dots, top-right of the post) → **Pin to top of Page**.
3. Confirm it now sits above everything with a "Pinned" label.
4. Only one post can be pinned; re-pin later when the message changes.

## 7. Promote → "Get more website visitors" (10 min)

1. Page → **Promote** (or **Boost** → **Get more website visitors**).
2. **Goal:** confirm *Get more website visitors* is selected. Not "Get more
   messages", not "Boost a post" — those optimise for the wrong action.
3. **Creative:** choose **Upload media** → `demo-money-mindset-30s.mp4`. Video
   outperforms a static image on this objective and you already have it.
   - If it asks for a thumbnail: `poster-30s-1280x720.jpg`.
4. **Text:** paste from `COPY.md` §5 — primary text, headline, description.
5. **Website URL:** `https://www.tolley.io/animate`
6. **Button:** `Learn more`.
7. **Audience:** click **Edit** on the automatic audience → **Create new
   audience**:
   - Location: **United States** (start here; widen later)
   - Age **25–54**, all genders
   - Detailed targeting: add 6–10 interests from `COPY.md` §5
   - Save the audience with a name like `JS — creators US 25-54`
8. **Duration and budget:** `$10.00` per day, `15` days → total `$150`.
   Facebook shows an estimated reach; ignore the optimism, watch the real
   numbers on day 3.
9. **AI-generated content:** toggle the disclosure **ON** (§8).
10. **Payment:** confirm the card on file is the business card, not personal.
11. **Promote now**.
12. Check back at 48 hours. If cost-per-link-click is above ~$1.50, swap in ad
    copy Option B or C from `COPY.md` §5 rather than raising the budget.

## 8. The AI-generated content disclosure

Facebook asks, on both organic video posts and ads, whether the content is
digitally created or altered. **Every Jelly Studio video is — always say yes.**

- **On a post:** in the composer, before posting, open the ⋯ / *More options*
  and switch on **AI-generated content** (sometimes labelled *Digitally created
  or altered*).
- **On an ad:** the same toggle appears in the Promote flow near the creative.

Declaring it costs nothing measurable in reach. Not declaring it risks the ad
being rejected and the Page taking a strike, which is much more expensive than
the label.

## What "Automated Ads", "Boost", and "Leads" mean — and why we skip them

| Facebook offers | What it actually is | Our call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automated Ads** (Meta Advantage / "always-on") | Meta picks the audience, creative and budget for you and keeps spending continuously. Good when you have no creative and no opinion. | **Skip.** We have one strong video and a specific audience thesis. Automated Ads would spread $10/day across placements we can't read, and you would learn nothing about which message works. |
| **Boost post** | Optimises for *post engagement* — likes, comments, shares. Cheap reactions, almost no clicks. | **Skip.** Engagement on a beta signup page is a vanity number. We want people to reach tolley.io/animate, which is why the objective is *Get more website visitors*. |
| **Get more leads / Lead form** | Collects name and email inside Facebook via an instant form. | **Skip for now.** A Facebook lead form drops people into a list we would then have to email, and cold outbound from tolley.io is deliberately blocked. The invite flow is a DM conversation on purpose — it's higher quality and it's already staffed by you. |
| **Get more messages** | Optimises for people opening Messenger. | **Not yet.** Worth revisiting *after* the website objective has data, since the DM-for-an-invite flow really is the conversion. Test it as a second campaign, not the first. |

One more thing to leave alone: Facebook will suggest **"Add a shop"** and
**"Add product variants"** on any Page that mentions prices. Don't. Jelly Studio
sells prepaid credit through Stripe on tolley.io, not through Facebook checkout.

## After it's live — a 5-minute weekly check

1. Page → **Professional dashboard** → reach, follows, link clicks.
2. Ads Manager → cost per link click and click-through rate on the promotion.
3. Count the invite DMs that actually turned into a signup. That is the only
   number that matters; everything above it is upstream of it.
