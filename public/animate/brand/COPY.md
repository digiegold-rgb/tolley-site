# Jelly Studio — Facebook Page copy deck

Everything below is paste-ready. Written **Aug 2026**; every dollar figure is a
reconciled render from `lib/vater/demo-videos.ts` on that date. If prices move
in the app, move them here too.

> **Positioning rule (Jared, Aug 2026).** Public copy leads with *affordability
> and no commitment*, never with the cost mechanic. **Do not put "compute at
> cost", "$0.35 per finished minute", or any per-minute rate on the Page, in a
> post, in the About section, or in an ad.** That breakdown belongs only on the
> in-app receipt and the Billing page, where someone is already a customer
> looking at their own charge. Publicly: *what a finished video came to*, never
> *how the number is built*.
>
> Also off-limits: "cheapest in the industry", "cheaper than <competitor>", or
> naming any competitor. **"The most affordable way"** and **"no commitment"**
> are the approved claims.

## 7. Voice and claim rules

Keep these true or the Page stops being worth trusting:

- **Lead with affordability and no commitment.** "The most affordable way to
  make faceless videos", "pay only for what you render", "no subscription, no
  commitment".
- **Never publish the cost mechanic.** No "compute at cost", no "$0.35 per
  finished minute", no per-minute rate of any kind on any public surface. The
  itemised receipt is an in-app screen for existing customers only.
- **Never say "cheapest in the industry" and never name a competitor.** Approved
  claim is "most affordable"; comparative superlatives against named rivals are
  a legal problem, not a copy problem.
- **Quote totals, and only real ones.** $0.73 / 0:29, $3.04 / 3:24, $4.49 / 8:38,
  $5.56 / 8:44 are reconciled renders from Aug 2026. "Most long-form videos cost
  $1–7" is the safe general claim.
- **"The Quiet Exit" (3:24, $3.04) is the only render cleared to show.** It is
  Jared's own script and voice. The other three are quoted as duration + total
  only — no titles, no frames, no posters — because their consent status is
  still pending in `demo-videos.ts`.
- **Say "public beta, invite only" and date it "Aug 2026."** Don't imply general
  availability. Never say "unlimited" — beta max length is 9:00.
- **Never call a generated character a real customer** or put a testimonial in a
  character's mouth.
- The videos are AI-generated. On any Facebook ad or Reel, **switch on the
  AI-generated content disclosure** — it is required and it costs nothing in
  reach compared to being caught not doing it.

| Labels & numbers | JetBrains Mono 400–700 | eyebrows, prices, URLs |

### The mark

A **jelly-wobble squircle** — an asymmetric rounded shape (uneven corner radii
give the soft bounce) filled with the product's own create-gradient, carrying a
bold white **J**. No droplet, no food imagery.

- `logo-1024.png` / `logo-512.png` — mark on the dark plate, for the profile
  picture. **Mark only, no wordmark:** at Facebook's 170px display a wordmark
  inside the square degrades to unreadable texture, and the avatar also gets
  circle-cropped. The J alone still reads at the 48px feed size.
- `logo-mark-transparent.png` — the same mark on transparency, for any ground.
- `logo-lockup-1600x400.png` — the horizontal wordmark lockup: mark +
  **JELLY·STUDIO** + **BY TOLLEY.IO**. Use this anywhere the name needs to be
  read — email headers, decks, slide footers. The same lockup is built into the
  cover, all four posts, the end cards and the Reels frame.
